### Contrast check

The UI uses the dark palette as its shipped mode. `#e5f7eb` on `#07110d` is the primary
body pair; muted text is used for metadata only. Accent colors are paired with dark panels
or dark text, never used as the sole indicator of a state.

## Typography - Space Grotesk + Space Mono

- **Display + body:** Space Grotesk Variable, self-hosted with `@fontsource-variable/space-grotesk`
- **Mono (numbers, addresses, code):** Space Mono, self-hosted with `@fontsource/space-mono`

The pairing follows the pack's bold/technical Pair E direction. All measured values use
tabular mono numerals; no live value uses scientific notation or an ellipsis.

### Type scale

| Role | Use |

| H1 | Screen title |

## Gradients (not used)

The workstation direction uses flat dark surfaces and semantic accent strokes. Gradients
would compete with the evidence hierarchy, so none are generated.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Direct, specific, number-forward, factual. Prefer captured, measured, readback, rejected,
admitted, unavailable, and verified.

### Words to avoid

Revolutionary, seamless, guaranteed, secure, unlock, and any claim not present in the
evidence JSON. Never turn an unavailable artifact into a success claim.

### Voice example

> `2,664,784 gas measured. MAX_VERIFY_GAS captured. Staked admission unavailable.`

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Keep the shell dense and left-aligned, with one clear primary action per screen.
- Use `Space Mono` with tabular numerals for every number, address, and raw response.
- Use dashed stamped empty states with the exact evidence reason.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and use spring motion only for staged reveals.

**Don't:**

- Add facts, links, quotes, or chain status that are not in `demoEvidence.json`.
- Use pure black, `transition: all`, decorative gradients, or competing accent colors.
- Present a missing mined transaction, PQ write, or rotation as if it exists.
